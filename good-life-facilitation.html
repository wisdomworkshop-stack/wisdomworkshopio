An early teacher of mine described the Tao Te Ching as a magic mirror. Group leader John Heider sees it as a powerful guide for wise leaders, or as a way to practice more effective psychotherapy.

Besides the technical skills, what are the human skills most important for culturing community?

What do we need? Like an Epicurean garden, we need three things:

  • a wall
  • good snacks
  • good people
  • and here, good philosophy
“All of this inner work is necessary and fulfilling… but it doesn’t mean anything unless we take it back out into the world. We have to dance it out into the world in some way.”Quinn, World Philosophy fellow, Fall 2026

That’s what this season is for. The inner work is meant to be shared, given back. If you’re tending a circle, a class, a writing group, a team, or a community you haven’t gathered yet, come practice the gentle art of holding it.

A doorway into the perMA

Good Life Facilitation is one way into the perMA, the Wisdom Workshop’s Personal Master of Arts: a person-centered alternative to graduate school, where you choose the path and your portfolio does the speaking. Come for these five weeks, or let them be the first steps of something longer.

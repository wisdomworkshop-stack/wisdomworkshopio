From past circles

Your facilitator

Sean Waters has taught writing at Colorado State University for 17 years and holds master’s degrees in Philosophy and English Education. He founded the Wisdom Workshop in 2021 and has led more than 1,500 hours of small-group facilitation with 115+ participants in 9 countries.

“Sean has a gift for warmth, genuineness, and unconditional positive regard.”

What you’ll leave with

  • A facilitation practice you’ve tested. Each week you write a short process letter on how you’re holding your own groups, toward an end-of-2026 goal.
  • A written commitment to the community you’re building, want to build, or want to contribute to.
  • A finals presentation to a circle that has watched you grow, followed by an exit conversation.

Our map is the three Taoist treasures (verse 67): simplicity → generosity, deep love → courage, equality → leadership.

The five weeks

We move through the Seven Ps of Good Life Writing, held in four relationships: Self, Life, Others, and World. That’s SLOW facilitation.

Dates below are for the Wednesday groups; the Tuesday group meets the day before.

  1. Nov 11Self

    Presence + Purpose

    Carl Rogers, A Way of Being

    Besides the technical skills, what are the human skills most important for culturing community?

    The three facilitative conditions of the helping relationship: unconditional positive regard, genuineness, and empathic listening. How do they apply to the communities we culture, the containers we tend, and the conversations we host? Your first process letter: your purpose, and the community you’re building.

  2. Nov 18Life

    Play

    Selections from John Heider, The Tao of Leadership

    How might you guard and keep the treasures of simplicity, deep love, and equality?

    An encounter-group leader’s reading of the Tao Te Ching: effortless action and non-violence. We center the three treasures of verse 67.

  3. Nov 24/25

    No meeting for Thanksgiving. Catch up, and dance your thanks.

  4. Dec 2Life

    Projects

    Marilyn M. Taylor, Emergent Learning for Wisdom

    How do we build the garden wall, serve good snacks, and gather good people to cultivate emergent learning for wisdom?

    The psychology of learning, the phases of emergent learning, and how leadership nurtures cultures where deep learning happens.

  5. Dec 9Others

    Personhood

    Reinhard Stelter, A Guide to Third Generation Coaching (PDF provided), with selections from Victor Turner, The Ritual Process

    Who are the people you want to learn with? What is third-generation coaching, and why does it matter now?

    Symmetry between I–Thou dialogue partners, and narrative, collaborative meaning-making: the foundation for one-on-ones and participatory facilitation. We’ll also draw comparisons to Turner’s work on ritual and communitas.

  6. Dec 16World

    Process + Perspective

    Selections from Irvin Yalom & Molyn Leszcz, The Theory and Practice of Group Psychotherapy

    What are the healing factors in any group? How do we culture healing environments that reach beyond the group itself?

    Yalom first identified the eleven therapeutic factors of groups. Prepare your finals presentation and exit conversation.

  7. Dec 16–18Finals

    Closing celebration

    What does it look like to dance your thanks?

    We celebrate how each of us has been culturing our own communities. Each participant shares their key learnings in an exit interview, a hosted presentation, or both.

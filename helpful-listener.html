What kind of helpful listener are you?

Six moments and eight questions. About ten minutes. You will leave with your first move, your home ground, and your growing edge.

Everyone who tries to help has a first move. It comes out before we choose it, and it usually comes from care.

Carl Rogers spent fifty years watching what happens after that first move. He found that people grow in a certain climate, and that the climate is made of three attitudes in the helper: being real, prizing the person as they are, and understanding them from the inside. Underneath all three is presence, the capacity to stay with someone without reaching in to fix them.

This is a mirror, and there are no wrong answers in it. Answer as you are on an ordinary Tuesday, not as you would like to be.

Built from Carl Rogers, A Way of Being (1980), and Elias Porter's five kinds of helper response (1950). It is a prompt for reflection, not a validated assessment.

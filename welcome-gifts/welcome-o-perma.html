Kindle. Ground. Connect. Deepen. Grow.

Welcome. You've entered a practice, not a course. Over ten sessions across five weeks, we'll move through the Wuxing five-element cycle — Fire kindles, Earth grounds, Metal connects, Water deepens, Wood grows — mapped to Martin Seligman's PERMA framework for human flourishing. This is how humans learn best: together, slowly, with care.

Before We Begin

Read

  • Writing Down the Bones by Natalie Goldberg — The foundational text on mindful freewriting. Read the first three chapters before session one.
  • Flourish by Martin Seligman — Chapter 1, "A New Theory of Well-Being," for the PERMA framework we'll be living inside.

Write

Find a notebook you love. Before our first gathering, spend ten minutes with this prompt:

"What do I already know about flourishing — from my own life, not from books? What has actually worked?"

Write freely. Don't edit. This is practice, not performance.

Listen

Pay attention, this week, to one conversation where you feel genuinely heard. Notice what the other person did. Notice what it felt like in your body. Bring that observation to our first session.

What to Expect

Sessions run 75 minutes. We begin with a mindful freewrite. We share what we wrote — not to perform, but to practice being seen. The room is capped at eight participants plus facilitator. There are no passive observers. You will be asked to speak, to listen, and to trust the group's collective intelligence.

Three commitments we hold: "I won't should on you if you don't should on me." "You do you." "Don't ick my wow."

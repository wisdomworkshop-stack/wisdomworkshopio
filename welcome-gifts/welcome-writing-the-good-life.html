Write to Learn Better, Live Better, and Love Better

Welcome. You've joined the waitlist for the 19th iteration of Writing the Good Life — seven weekly gatherings dedicated to the practice of turning attention into prose. This isn't a writing workshop in the usual sense. We don't critique each other's work. We write together, share what we wrote, and listen to each other with real attention. The quality of the listening changes the quality of the writing.

Before We Begin

Read

  • Writing Down the Bones by Natalie Goldberg (1986) — The practice of freewriting as a path to knowing yourself. Start anywhere. Read with a pen in your hand.
  • The Artist's Way by Julia Cameron (1992) — Chapters 1–2, on Morning Pages and Artist's Dates. We'll be using both.
  • Designing Your Life by Bill Burnett & Dave Evans (2016) — For later in the course, when we move into life design and Odyssey Plans.

Write

Begin Morning Pages this week. Three pages, longhand, first thing in the morning. Stream of consciousness. No one reads them, including you (at least not yet). This is the practice we'll be building on together.

Then, when you're ready, try this prompt:

"How can I use writing to clarify and cultivate the conditions for human flourishing — starting with my own?"

Listen

This week, notice one moment where you're fully present. Not multitasking. Not planning the next thing. Just here. Write down what you noticed — even a single sentence is enough.

The Arc

Seven weeks, seven thresholds: Presence, Purpose, Play, Projects, People, Process, Perspective. Each week builds a new framework for writing as a practice of flourishing. Each week includes a workbook with seven writing invitations and experiential encouragement. By the end, you'll have a Draft Creative Contract, a Coherence Compass, an Odyssey Plan, and a Renewed Creative Contract. More importantly, you'll have a writing practice that nourishes you.
